What can you demonstrate?

Turn measured practice into an honest skills profile, resume wording, and interview preparation. XP measures activity; this page measures completed exam performance.

Practice evidence, not certification or work experience.

These local, self-administered results cannot prove an unaided attempt, identity, job readiness, or Cisco certification. Do not list "CCNA certified" unless you actually earned it. Simulated CLI exercises are not production experience. There is no universal official Cisco passing score claimed here.

Assessment checkpoint

Complete a full 100-question exam with the 120-minute countdown enabled, keep answers hidden throughout, and score at least 80% before rounding. The newest attempt under those conditions controls this profile: a later failed assessment pauses resume suggestions until you pass again.

Knowledge coverage and gaps

Based on your newest full, timed, hidden-feedback assessment. A concept needs at least 3 questions and 80% correct to support a conceptual-knowledge suggestion. Unsampled concepts are not assessed; one correct answer is not mastery.

Concept groups are mapped automatically from question wording and topics. They are a practice-study aid, not a validated competency taxonomy or a comprehensive assessment of the whole subject.
